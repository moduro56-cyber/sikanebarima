Affidavit Assistance

Need help preparing affidavit documentation? We assist customers with understanding the information required and preparing documentation for the appropriate process.

Types of Assistance

  • Name-related affidavit documentation.
  • Date-of-birth-related affidavit documentation.
  • School sponsorship affidavit documentation.
  • Guidance for customers applying from outside Ghana.

What You May Need

  • Valid identification, where required.
  • Accurate personal details and supporting documents.
  • The purpose for which the affidavit is needed.
  • Any additional information required by the authorised officer.
Important: Requirements depend on the purpose of the affidavit. Swearing, witnessing and certification must be completed by the appropriately authorised person where required. Do not sign an affidavit before being instructed to do so by the authorised officer.
