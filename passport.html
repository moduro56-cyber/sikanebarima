Passport Application Assistance

Need help preparing your passport application? SIKA NE BARIMA LEGAL CHAMBER provides documentation assistance and guidance through the application process.

Passport Requirements

Requirements can depend on whether you are applying for a first passport, renewing a passport, or replacing a lost or damaged passport.

  • Identify the correct passport application category.
  • Check the current official document checklist.
  • Prepare the supporting information and documents requested.
  • Follow the official application and appointment instructions.
Important: Confirm the latest requirements, fees, appointment procedures and processing times with Ghana's official passport authority. Requirements may change. Passport issuance is determined by the relevant authority.
