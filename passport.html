Passport Requirements

Requirements depend on whether you are applying for a first passport, renewing a passport, or replacing a lost or damaged passport.

  • Identify the correct passport application category.
  • Check the current official document checklist.
  • Prepare the supporting information and documents requested.
  • Follow the official application and appointment instructions.
Important: Confirm the latest requirements, official fees, appointment procedures and processing times with Ghana's official passport authority. Requirements can change, and passport issuance is determined by the relevant authority.
