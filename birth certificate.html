Birth Certificate Assistance

Need assistance with birth certificate documentation? We help customers understand the relevant application process and prepare the information required for their case.

Information to Prepare

  • Your reason for applying or requesting a correction.
  • Available birth registration details and supporting records.
  • Identification and other documents required for your case.
  • Any additional information requested by the relevant authority.
Important: Requirements differ depending on the application, registration status or correction requested. Confirm the current checklist, official fees and processing times with the appropriate Ghanaian authority. Certificate issuance and approval are determined by that authority.
